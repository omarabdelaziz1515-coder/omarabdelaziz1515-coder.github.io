Automations and analysis

Smaller tools that replaced manual work. Each one runs on a schedule and lands in someone’s inbox.

Campaign conversions engine

Finds every SMS campaign file across a shared Drive folder tree, matches recipients to sales, and scores same-day, 3-day and 7-day conversion in a styled daily email.

Google Apps Script, BigQuery, Google Drive API

Daily growth actions

A daily email of call lists for telesales and customer service, cleaned of customers who hit checkout errors so agents only call people they can help.

Google Apps Script, BigQuery

Delinquency deep analysis

Weekly report on how customers move into 30+, 60+ and 90+ day buckets, focused on the last 30 days so Risk can act on it.

SQL, Google Apps Script

Cohort growth report

Month-over-month view of every customer cohort since launch, rewritten from a data dump into a management overview with growth insights.

BigQuery, n8n

1st to 5th transaction dashboard

Tracks how customers move from their first purchase to their fifth, with revenue joined in from merchant settlements and caching to keep it fast.

Google Apps Script, BigQuery

Monthly campaign calendar

A planning sheet that turns merchant offers and promo codes into each month’s SMS calendar, then into daily batch files in Drive.

Google Sheets, Apps Script

SMS campaign attribution

Matched campaign recipients to same-day sales after normalising Egyptian mobile number formats, and packaged the results into a multi-tab Excel workbook.

Python, pandas, Excel

Down-payment error analysis

A 12-query SQL investigation into checkout down-payment errors, delivered as a 10-tab workbook the team could filter by cause.

BigQuery SQL, Excel
